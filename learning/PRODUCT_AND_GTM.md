# BUSINESS IN PLAY — Brand, Product, SEO and Market Test (working v0.1)

**Status:** research/validation proposal, not a live commercial launch. Brand name is a placeholder until UK and EU trademark/domain clearance; do not purchase a domain or imply registered rights solely from this document.

## Brand architecture
**Parent platform (provisional):** BUSINESS IN PLAY  
**Tagline:** *Make the call. Live the story. Learn what matters.*  
**Product portfolio:** 
- **Opening Night** — a lighter, sociable business event story (current six-scene prototype)
- **The 09:17 Incident** — the existing, more serious experimental strategy/crisis scenario, retained under the old BUSINESS CRISIS project for comparison
- Future: a negotiation-focused scenario, a marketing-focused scenario, and a service-operations scenario, only if users ask for them

**Reason:** a parent name that suggests business *and* participation serves HR, agencies, trainers and universities better than a headline name dominated by stress or catastrophe. Working titles must undergo proper trademark/domain/confusion checks with a professional before money is spent.

## The buying decision is different for each customer

| Buyer | Their job to be done | Proposed first offer | Sales evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR / People & Culture | Run a memorable event for ~25 colleagues with minimal admin | One-off facilitated event with simple joining, dedicated projector, optional discussion and local report | 25-device real-world tests, actual duration, satisfaction, credible host procedures |
| Team-building agencies | Deliver repeatable sessions across multiple clients | Agency partner pilot with per-session licensing, separate facilitator manual, limited reusable story catalogue | Repeatable quality, reliable white-label boundaries, support terms, margin per session |
| Leadership trainers | Facilitate discussions about decision-making, motivation, influence | Trainer edition with lesson cards, scenario notes and debrief, replayable cohorts | Evidence that reflection improves the session, trainer time estimates, clear coach value |
| Universities/colleges | Teach specific outcomes in a module seminar | Lecturer pilot mapped to Unit 20 Organisational Behaviour Management, plus a separate optional individual reflection task | Lecturer review, mapped outcomes, no fabricated assessment approval, student usability/learning evidence |

**No one-size-fits-all pricing:** test value and operating costs independently in each segment. A facilitated event includes human delivery; a software licence does not. Avoid a single permanent per-person number until the workload and technical capability are known.

## Positioning
**What it is:** a collection of original narrative business experiences combining group decisions, limited information, accountable trade-offs and a debrief that links actions to consequences.  
**What it is not:** a generic quiz, an accredited assessment, a psychometric test, a substitute for a full degree, or an unhosted enterprise platform today.

**Customer-facing proposition for HR:**
"Bring colleagues together around a live story. Teams debate, make the call and see what happens next."
**For a lecturer:**
"Turn a module concept into a decision students can defend, compare and reflect on."
**For an agency:**
"Add a repeatable story-led team challenge to your events catalogue without building the decision engine."
**For a trainer:**
"Use realistic dilemmas as the starting point for better conversations about judgement and teamwork."

These are **copy hypotheses**, not tested customer messaging.

## Commercial rollout — gated, not date promises

### Gate A: product clarity
- Join flow must be explainable in under 30 seconds during a real trial.
- Demonstrate full game on the actual intended devices: no broken team selection, silent failures or inaccessible controls.
- Distinguish shared-device preview from a live room product. Current Opening Night is single-device ONLY.

### Gate B: enjoyment and educational relevance
- At least five independently recruited moderated groups with a clear mix: 2 social/HR-like, 1 agency/trainer, 2 classroom/learner.
- Ask: enjoyment 1–5, clarity 1–5, desire to play again, useful discussion generated, most confusing moment, willingness to recommend. Do not equate a score with real skills.
- Independent lecturer checks Unit 20 LO1–LO4 mapping. Collect the actual assignment/rubric only when permitted; do not promise that gameplay earns a grade.

### Gate C: real-world infrastructure
- Load/reconnect test for at least 25 physical phones across typical venue Wi-Fi. The older room engine has a software input ceiling of 30 but no field proof.
- Secure host identity/recovery, capacity limits, rate limiting, privacy notices, retention/deletion, export privacy, genuine projector and iOS download testing.
- A trained host runs three uninterrupted practice events and can recover after connectivity loss.

### Gate D: first commercial pilots
- Offer a limited number of clearly labelled pilot sessions to qualified buyers.
- Measure participant completion, real facilitation hours, booking conversion, repeat interest, total costs and contribution margin.
- Collect actual testimonials only with permission; no fabricated clients or guaranteed enjoyment claims.

## Suggested offer experiments (NOT published prices)
- HR: hosted event vs self-facilitated licensing; test the acceptable fee *after* reliability proof.
- Agency: per-session wholesale model vs annual minimum + support tier.
- Trainer: quarterly pilot pack with facilitator support vs cohort licence.
- Education: per-seminar pilot, per-module licence or campus licence; separate student and academic staff access where necessary.

Do not copy competitor prices into our public pages as though equivalent. Hosting, number of facilitators, customisation, duration and event capacity change the economics.

### SEO release controls
- Keep all experimental pages `noindex`; do not add staging pages to sitemap.
- Once a final public origin and canonical URL are configured, use unique human-readable title/description/H1 by audience, descriptive internal links, helpful FAQ content, OG image and truthful structured data.
- No claims of Harvard certification or Pearson endorsement. Cite their public pedagogical/specification material with honest attribution, do not imply affiliation.
- No fake testimonials, fake star ratings, schema for non-existent offers or inflated completion/availability.
- Google Search Console verification and sitemap submission should follow the approved domain launch (the founder already planned a later reminder for existing project SEO).

## First 90-day validation experiment
**Days 1–14:** engineering QA and three facilitator rehearsals; record every confusion point and recovery action.  
**Days 15–30:** pilot five independent groups and interview five actual prospective buyers from the four segments. Choose one primary offer based on evidence.  
**Days 31–60:** run 3–5 transparently priced pilots if technical/legal release criteria are passed. Test two marketing messages, not a dozen simultaneous promises.  
**Days 61–90:** publish the approved demo, documented operating system, privacy/terms, case study(s) and segment-specific offer; pursue carefully selected agencies/educators with opt-out-respecting outreach.

Targets are **experiments**, not predictions or guarantees.
