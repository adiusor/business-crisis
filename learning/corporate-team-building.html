QUESTIONS / HONEST ANSWERS

Before you plan a session.

Can 25 people already join on their own phones?

Not yet for Opening Night. The new story has a single-device game and a separately tested server-authoritative core; the complete phone-room UI and physical-device load test are not finished.

Is this another leadership assessment?

No. The social format focuses on narrative, teamwork and enjoyment. Reflective learning is available but optional.

Can we pay and book an event?

No public bookings or binding prices are available during this prototype stage.
