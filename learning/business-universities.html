LEARNING OUTCOMES FIRST

Designed around your goal.

Our initial instructor materials map Opening Night to Pearson BTEC Higher Nationals Business Unit 20, Organisational Behaviour Management. Other module connections—including operations and negotiation—are supplementary; they are not claims of broad degree accreditation.

01

LO1 / Individual differences

Reflect on perception, assumptions and how different viewpoints affect management relationships. Gameplay alone does not assess personal reflection.

02

LO2 / Motivation

Compare pay, staff autonomy, voice and fairness against content and process motivational theories.

03

LO3 & LO4 / Teams, power and culture

Run a group decision activity, observe collaboration and examine sponsor influence, informal power and the culture that silences staff.

QUESTIONS / HONEST ANSWERS

Before you plan a session.

Is the case from Harvard Business School?

No. Opening Night is original. Harvard's public case-method guidance is used only as methodological inspiration, not as a claim of affiliation or a licensed case.

Does completing the game earn Pearson Unit 20 credit?

No. The mapping is a teaching aid only; assessment must follow the institution's published brief and marking rules.

Can I run this tomorrow in a live class?

The single-device experimental preview can be reviewed. The format, timing and separate-phone multiplayer must be tested before promising a classroom delivery experience.
