FACILITATOR: Read these six rules aloud before starting the first episode.

  1. 01
    SIX EPISODES. ONE CRISIS.

    Your decisions affect everything that follows.

  2. 02
    CHOOSE YOUR INTELLIGENCE.

    Your captain selects two advisers. Their advice stays confidential to your team.

  3. 03
    EVERY VOICE COUNTS.

    Discuss together. Each player locks one complete response: Strategy, Public Statement and any optional investment.

  4. 04
    THE MAJORITY DECIDES.

    Only participation progress is visible until everyone votes. A tie triggers a private revote; a second tie goes to the captain's ballot.

  5. 05
    THE CLOCK IS REAL.

    3:00 without penalty; one reserve minute costs −5 points. No final answer by 4:00 costs −12 plus economic damage. Your team clock stops on a confirmed decision.

  6. 06
    SURVIVE TO WIN.

    Cash, trust and risks carry forward. A champion needs a renewed client contract, solvent funds and viable operations — not just points.
